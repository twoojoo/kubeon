Kubeon vs Lens

Lens made the Kubernetes IDE popular, and for good reasons: it is polished, has extensions and built-in metrics charts. Kubeon is a younger, native alternative: a single Go binary with a GPU-rendered UI on the k9s engine, free software with no account.

Where Kubeon is the better fit

  • No Electron, no account. One native process that reads your kubeconfig and talks to your clusters, nothing else.
  • The k9s engine. Same columns, colors, filters and keys as k9s, live from informers, with virtualized tables.
  • Logs of a whole workload. One stream for every pod of a deployment, JSON pretty-printed, filtered by level.
  • Production guard rails. Protected contexts require typing the resource name; read-only contexts block changes in the UI.
  • Free software. GPL-3.0, with no paid edition.

Where Lens is the better fit

  • You rely on Lens extensions or on its Prometheus metrics charts.
  • You use Lens team or enterprise features.
  • You prefer a tool with a long track record in large teams.
